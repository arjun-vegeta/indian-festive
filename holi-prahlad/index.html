होली · Holi

Why we celebrate Holi

the festival of colours, at the end of winter

  • Holi celebrates good over evil, and Prahlad's faith. The fire could not hurt him.
  • The night before is Holika Dahan. People light a bonfire, walk round it and offer new grain.
  • Holi comes on the full moon of Phalguna. Spring is here, and the new wheat is almost ready to cut.
  • In Braj, around Mathura and Vrindavan, Holi remembers Radha and Krishna's colour play. In Barsana, women chase the men in fun with sticks: Lathmar Holi.
  • Holi is a day to forgive, forget old quarrels and start fresh. "Bura na mano, Holi hai!" means "Don't mind, it's Holi!"
