Why we celebrate Durga Puja

the goddess who comes home every autumn

  • The gods put their light together, and Durga was born. She defeated Mahishasura, the buffalo demon, and gave heaven back. Good wins over evil.
  • Navaratri means "nine nights". It ends on Vijaya Dashami, the same day as Dussehra. Rama's island is right next door.
  • In Bengal, Durga Puja is a homecoming. Every autumn Durga comes home to her parents, with her children Lakshmi, Saraswati, Ganesha and Kartikeya.
  • Every neighbourhood builds a pandal. There are dhak drums, the dhunuchi dance, and flowers for pushpanjali.
  • On the last day, married women say goodbye with sindoor khela. Then the pratima goes to the river, and everyone calls "Asche bochor abar hobe!": it will come again next year.
  • In Gujarat, people dance garba and dandiya all nine nights. In the south, families set out golu dolls on steps.
