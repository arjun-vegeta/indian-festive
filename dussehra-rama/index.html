विजयादशमी · Vijayadashami

Why we celebrate Dussehra

the tenth day, the day of victory

  • Vijayadashami means "the tenth day of victory". It comes right after the nine nights of Navaratri.
  • In the north, it marks Rama's victory over Ravana. Towns act out the Ramayana as the Ramlila, and burn Ravana at dusk: the Ravana Dahan.
  • In Bengal and the east, these days are Durga Puja: Durga's victory over Mahishasura. That is another story, on another island.
  • In the south, families keep Ayudha Puja. They honour the tools of their work: books, ploughs, machines, even bicycles and cars.
  • In Mysuru, Dasara is a royal festival. The palace glows with lights, and an elephant carries the goddess Chamundeshwari through the city.
  • Friends and neighbours swap shami or apta leaves as "gold", and wish each other well.
  • Every story says the same thing: good wins over evil.
