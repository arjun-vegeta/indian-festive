Why we celebrate Diwali

Deepavali means "a row of lamps".

  • In the north, Diwali remembers the night Rama came home to Ayodhya. The city lit rows of lamps to light his way.
  • In the south, many celebrate Krishna and Satyabhama's victory over Narakasura, on Naraka Chaturdashi. That is another story, on another island.
  • On Amavasya, the moonless night, families do Lakshmi Puja to welcome Lakshmi, goddess of wealth and well-being.
  • In Bengal, the same night is Kali Puja.
  • Sikhs celebrate Bandi Chhor Divas, when Guru Hargobind was freed from Gwalior Fort and came home to Amritsar.
  • Jains remember the night Mahavira attained nirvana.
  • Diwali runs over five days: Dhanteras, Naraka Chaturdashi, Lakshmi Puja, Govardhan Puja and Bhai Dooj.
